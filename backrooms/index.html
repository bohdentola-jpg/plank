HOW TO SURVIVE

THE CAMERA

  • R — record / pause. The tape only advances while recording.
  • N — NIGHTSHOT. Sees in the dark, drinks the battery twice as fast.
  • SCROLL — zoom. Useful for looking at something you should not.
  • F / right mouse — torch. Its own battery. D-cells refill both.

THE BODY

  • WASD move · SHIFT run (loud) · CTRL crouch (quiet)
  • SPACE hop a ledge · water: SPACE up, CTRL down
  • E take / read / use a door · Q use item · G throw it
  • 1–5 / TAB pick an item · ESC pause

THE RULES OTHERS WROTE DOWN

  1. Noise is the whole game. Most of what lives here is blind and none of it is deaf.
  2. Light stops some things and calls others. Learn which is which per level.
  3. If a thing is smiling in the dark, put light on it and do not stop putting light on it.
  4. If a thing has no face, don't keep looking at it.
  5. If a thing hasn't moved and is closer, do not look away.
  6. Almond water is real and it does help. Nobody knows why.
  7. Every level has a way out. It is never signposted, and it is never where the level wants you to be.

Nerve falls in the dark, near things that hunt, and when the tape damages. At zero the walls start moving on their own. Read the pages you find — that helps too.
