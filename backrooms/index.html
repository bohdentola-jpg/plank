THE ARCHIVE
HOW TO SURVIVE

THE RUN

  • Each floor has one service lift. Find it and you are off the floor.
  • Somebody chalked arrows toward the lift. Follow them. You can hear it humming when you get close.
  • Each floor has one thing living on it. It kills you on contact. There is no health bar.
  • There is always somewhere to hide, and it is different on every floor. [E] gets you in.
  • In the lift there is a stall. It takes footage and sells you the rest of the run.

THE CAMERA

  • R — record / pause. No tape running, no footage earned.
  • N — NIGHTSHOT. Sees in the dark, eats the battery.
  • F / right mouse — torch. It is the only thing you carry.
  • Keep the thing in frame from a distance while recording and the stall pays for it.

THE BODY

  • WASD move · SHIFT run (loud) · CTRL crouch (quiet)
  • SPACE hop a ledge · in water: SPACE up, CTRL down
  • E hide / come out / take the lift · ESC pause

WHAT OTHERS WORKED OUT

  1. Noise is the whole game. Most of what lives here is blind. None of it is deaf.
  2. Hiding works. It works much better if it did not see you get in.
  3. If it is smiling in the dark, keep the torch on it and do not stop.
  4. If it has no face, don't keep looking at it.
  5. If it hasn't moved and is closer, don't look away.
  6. Every floor has one trick that belongs to it. Learn it on the way in, not on the way out.

Nothing carries between runs except what you know. The stall resets, the floors are drawn fresh, and the way out is never where the floor wants you to be.

FOUND ON THE INLAY CARD, IN BIRO: CTRL + SHIFT + X — shows you the thing and the lift through the walls, from anywhere on the floor. It works. It also takes the game away from you.
